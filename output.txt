- Hardened vs Non-Hardened
Hardened keys: cannot derive child private keys from public key, more secure but slightly slower to generate.
Non-Hardened keys: child keys derivable from public key, convenient for watch-only wallets, less secure.

- Deterministic vs Non-Deterministic
Deterministic wallets: generate addresses from single seed, easier backup and recovery.
Non-Deterministic wallets: generate addresses randomly, backup each key individually. I you lose backup you lose funds.
